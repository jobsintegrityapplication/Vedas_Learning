## Acquisition requirements

Before importing any full text or recording, register exact edition/version, stable URL or bibliographic citation, publisher/performer provenance, access/retrieval date, license text or written permission (or a documented link-only decision), source file checksum, and reviewer. For audio, also record the exact text address range, recitation form, profile (`Kanchi` or `Sringeri`), performer/teacher, and alignment review. A checksum proves file identity/integrity only; it does not establish authenticity, completeness, correctness, or permission.

`content/ingestion/pages.jsonl`, `pages.csv`, `mantra_candidates.jsonl`, `mantra_candidates.csv`, `postgres_import.sql`, and `validation.json` are staging extractions, not approved canonical text. The run produced 4,130 page records and 21,191 candidate rows, including 38 Kanchi and 39 Sringeri whole-page fallback candidates without an explicit verse marker. It found 10,557 and 10,535 explicit verse-number candidates by profile; these counts are close to the expected corpus count but do not establish completeness. 3,287 Kanchi and 3,557 Sringeri marker candidates have ambiguous Sukta references and remain unassigned. Page-level source strings are preserved with method, confidence, page provenance, and uncertainty flags. SaiVeda describes RCCS as “Roman Coloured Coding Script” and describes color/vertical offsets as recitation cues ([RCCS explanation](https://www.saiveda.net/en/rccs.html)); the pipeline records non-black glyph color and position cues separately. The user requested an IAST candidate too; source roman text is preserved as-is and is not silently converted or certified as IAST. As of this register, no local Tesseract executable/Hindi language data is available; pages with unreliable embedded text are flagged for OCR and manual review.

SaiVeda's published RCCS legend maps black to the middle note, red to the upper note, blue to the lower note, and green to the lowest note, with vertical placement also encoding melody. The staging file stores raw RGB values and page coordinates; it does not infer a numeric pitch or convert the notation to svara names.
